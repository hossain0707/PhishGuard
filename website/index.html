PROTECTION ENGINE

Explainable assessments while you browse.

01

Rule-based detection

Weighted indicators examine HTTPS, suspicious keywords, IP-based domains, unsafe password forms and embedded content.

02

Decisive risk response

Low-risk pages continue normally, medium-risk pages show a choice, and high-risk pages redirect to a warning with no proceed option. Pages may load before detection.

03

Privacy by design

Browsing logs remain on your device. The online database holds only profile and authenticated browser-session data.

04

Monthly PDF reports

Export readable monthly PDF reports containing scan totals, threat levels and account-separated local assessments. Export from the extension dashboard.
