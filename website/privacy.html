Privacy and limitations

Updated 12 October 2026. PhishGuard is a student FYP prototype for rule-based browser risk assessment.

Account data

Email, display name, authentication data and browser installation registrations are processed by the configured Supabase service. Passwords are managed by Supabase Auth. Extension tokens stay in browser-session memory; restarting the browser requires logging in again. The website uses local browser storage for its session. Local guest-mode scanning does not need an account or send scanning history to the cloud.

Browsing assessments

While installed, the extension reads the URL and counts of password inputs, forms and embedded frames. It does not read passwords, form contents or page text. URLs without queries, fragments or credentials, scores, reasons and timestamps remain locally stored and separated by account. Paths may still contain personal information. Up to 5,000 assessments are retained; records older than 366 days are removed on the next scan. Browsing history is not uploaded or sold.

Your controls

Log out to leave account mode; local guest monitoring remains active. Erase your local history through the extension controls, or delete a selected month in Reports. Uninstalling deletes extension storage. Exported PDFs remain on your device until you delete them. To request account deletion or report a privacy concern, contact the project team using the contact supplied with your installation.

Limitations

Heuristics can produce false positives and false negatives. HTTPS and low scores do not prove safety. Redirects happen after loading; they cannot guarantee prevention of requests, downloads or credential theft. Dynamic changes are monitored with rate limits. Browser internal pages are excluded.

Release status

Development packages are for testing. Store approval, Android device tests, live database verification and operator contact details are required before public release. GMI identifies the academic context and does not imply institutional endorsement.
